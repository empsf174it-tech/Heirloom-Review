The 4 C's Diamond Guide

An interactive explainer for Cut, Color, Clarity, and Carat. Learn how to prioritize attributes based on your budget and preferences.

Cut

The most important factor for brilliance. A poor cut makes a diamond look dull regardless of its color or clarity.

Maximizes light return. Highly recommended.

Color

Ranges from D (colorless) to Z (light yellow). Near-colorless (G-J) offers great value while appearing white.

Appears colorless face-up. Best value.

Clarity

Measures inclusions. VS1-VS2 are "eye-clean" meaning flaws aren't visible without a loupe.

Eye-clean. Excellent balance of quality and price.

Carat

Refers to weight (1 ct = 0.2g), not physical dimensions, though they correlate. A round brilliant 1ct is roughly 6.5mm.

~6.5mm (1ct Round)

Natural vs. Lab-Grown

Lab-grown diamonds share the identical chemical and optical properties of natural diamonds. They offer significant cost savings for the same visual impact. Natural diamonds carry traditional heritage and rarity. The choice depends entirely on personal values and budget.

Certifications

A certificate (like GIA or IGI) maps the stone's exact attributes. Always verify the physical stone against the certificate plot, especially for inclusions.
