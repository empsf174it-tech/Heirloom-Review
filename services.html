The Heirloom Buying Checklist

Before making any fine jewelry or watch purchase, ensure you've checked these critical boxes.

  • 1. Verify Authenticity & Certifications

    Always request original papers. For diamonds, insist on reports from reputable labs like GIA or AGS. Match the laser inscription to the report.

  • 2. Understand the Warranty

    Check what is covered (movement issues, structural integrity) and what isn't (normal wear, water damage). Know the warranty duration.

  • 3. Confirm the Return Policy

    Is there a restocking fee? Can customized or sized items be returned? Ensure you have at least 14 days to inspect the piece in different lighting.

  • 4. Plan for Insurance

    Acquire an appraisal (separate from a grading report) for replacement value and add the item to a dedicated jewelry policy immediately.
