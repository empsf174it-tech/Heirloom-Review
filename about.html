Our Story

A fictional demo brand dedicated to the art of objective appraisal.

Heirloom Review was established to bring transparency to the often opaque worlds of haute horlogerie and fine jewelry. We believe that an heirloom should not just be a symbol of status, but a triumph of engineering and art.

Editorial Independence Statement: Our reviews are conducted independently. While we may earn affiliate commissions through our "Check price" links, these commissions never influence our scoring. We do not accept payment for favorable reviews.

How We Rate

Our rigorous 10-point scale separates hype from heritage.

Watch Criteria (/10)

  • Accuracy: Timekeeping variance over 7 days in 5 positions.
  • Finish: Hand-beveling, polishing, and dial details.
  • Comfort: Ergonomics, weight distribution, and clasp design.
  • Value: What you get relative to the asking price.
  • Service: Warranty length and manufacturer service reputation.

Jewelry Criteria (/10)

  • Craftsmanship: Setting security, symmetry, and finish.
  • Stone Quality: Verified against demo certificates for the 4 C's.
  • Comfort: Everyday wearability and snag-resistance.
  • Durability: Metal density and structural integrity.
  • Value: Material cost versus markup.
